# Habeas User Guide

**Version**: 2.0 (Multi-Profile Architecture)
**Last Updated**: January 2025

## Overview

Habeas is a mobile application that connects people facing potential immigration detention with legal representatives who can file emergency habeas corpus petitions. The app features a human-centered design with simplified signup and multi-profile support for families.

## Getting Started

### Unified Signup Process

The app uses a **unified signup flow** designed to be empathetic and easy to understand during stressful situations.

#### Step 1: Getting Help

From the home screen, tap **"Get Help"** to begin the signup process.

#### Step 2: Choose Your Situation

You'll be presented with two options that describe your situation:

**Option 1: "I'm worried that ICE may detain me or a loved one"**
- Choose this if you or someone you care about might face detention
- This will create a client helper account with profile management capabilities

**Option 2: "I am an attorney willing to file a habeas petition"**
- Choose this if you're a legal professional offering services
- This will create an attorney account

#### Step 3: Create Your Account

Enter your email address and create a secure password:
- **Email**: Must be a valid email address
- **Password**: Must be at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, and numbers

#### Step 4: Provide Details

Based on your selection, you'll provide different information:

**For Client Helpers:**
- Your personal information (name, birth date, etc.)
- Information about family members you want to help
- Emergency contact details

**For Attorneys:**
- Your name and contact information
- Your location (state and ZIP code)
- Professional details

#### Step 5: Confirmation

Review your information and complete the account creation.

## Multi-Profile Management

### What are Profiles?

If you chose "I'm worried that ICE may detain me or a loved one," you can create **multiple client profiles** under one account. This allows you to manage information for:
- Yourself
- Family members
- Friends or other people you want to help

### Creating Profiles

During signup, you can create profiles for multiple people:

1. **Profile Name**: Give each profile a descriptive name (e.g., "Myself", "My Brother", "My Mother")
2. **Is This You?**: Mark whether the profile represents yourself or someone else
3. **Personal Information**: Enter the person's details (name, birth date, country of birth, etc.)

### Managing Profiles

After creating your account, you can:
- **Add New Profiles**: Create additional profiles for other family members
- **Edit Profiles**: Update information as needed
- **Delete Profiles**: Remove profiles that are no longer needed
- **View All Profiles**: See a list of all people you're helping

## Using the App

### For Client Helpers

**Emergency Situations:**
1. Open the app
2. Select the profile of the person who needs help
3. Use the emergency slider to quickly connect with legal help
4. The app will automatically share the person's information with available attorneys

**Managing Information:**
- Keep profiles updated with current information
- Add emergency contacts for each person
- Review and update details regularly

### For Attorneys

**Receiving Cases:**
- You'll be notified when someone needs help in your area
- Review the case details and decide whether to accept
- Contact the client or their family directly

**Managing Your Profile:**
- Keep your contact information current
- Update your availability status
- Manage your court admissions and jurisdictions

## Privacy and Security

### Data Protection

- **No Data Collection Before Emergency**: The app doesn't collect or store your information until you actively request help
- **Secure Storage**: All information is encrypted and securely stored
- **Limited Sharing**: Information is only shared with attorneys when you request help

### What Information is Shared

When you request help, the following information may be shared with attorneys:
- The person's basic information (name, birth date, etc.)
- Location information (if provided)
- Emergency contact details
- Any additional details you've provided

### Controlling Your Data

- **Profile Management**: You can edit or delete profiles at any time
- **Account Deletion**: You can delete your entire account if needed
- **Data Requests**: You can request a copy of your data or ask for it to be deleted

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Do I need to create separate accounts for each family member?**
A: No! You can create one account and manage multiple profiles for different family members.

**Q: Can I use the app if I'm not sure about detention?**
A: Yes, you can create profiles and keep information ready even if detention isn't imminent.

**Q: Is the app available in other languages?**
A: Currently, the app is available in English. We're working on adding Spanish and other languages.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What devices does the app work on?**
A: The app works on both iOS and Android devices.

**Q: Do I need internet access to use the app?**
A: You need internet access to create accounts and request help, but you can view stored information offline.

**Q: How do I update my information?**
A: You can edit profiles anytime by going to your profile management section.

### Legal Questions

**Q: How quickly can an attorney respond?**
A: Our goal is to connect you with an attorney within one hour of your request.

**Q: What if no attorneys are available in my area?**
A: The app will try to find the closest available attorney and may connect you with someone in a nearby jurisdiction.

**Q: Is there a cost for using the app?**
A: The app itself is free. Individual attorneys may have their own fee structures.

## Account Management

### Updating Your Account

**Changing Your Email:**
1. Go to Account Settings
2. Select "Change Email"
3. Enter your new email address
4. Verify the change through the confirmation email

**Changing Your Password:**
1. Go to Account Settings
2. Select "Change Password"
3. Enter your current password and new password
4. Confirm the change

**Updating Profile Information:**
1. Go to Profile Management
2. Select the profile you want to update
3. Edit the information as needed
4. Save your changes

### Deleting Your Account

If you need to delete your account:
1. Go to Account Settings
2. Select "Delete Account"
3. Confirm your decision
4. All your data will be permanently removed

**Note**: Deleting your account cannot be undone. Make sure you want to permanently remove all your information.

## Tips for Effective Use

### Keeping Information Current

- **Regular Updates**: Review and update profile information monthly
- **Emergency Contacts**: Keep emergency contact information current
- **Location Information**: Update addresses if people move

### Preparing for Emergencies

- **Complete Profiles**: Fill out as much information as possible ahead of time
- **Test the App**: Familiarize yourself with the emergency features
- **Share Access**: Consider sharing account access with trusted family members

### Working with Attorneys

- **Be Responsive**: Respond quickly to attorney communications
- **Provide Documentation**: Have important documents ready to share
- **Ask Questions**: Don't hesitate to ask attorneys about the process
